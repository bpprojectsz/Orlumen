# Privacy Policy
Effective November 1, 2026
Orlumen (“the app”) turns the sound on your phone into edge lights and an always-on clock. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app has no accounts, no analytics of its own, and never records, stores or uploads your audio.
## What stays on your device
- Audio. Orlumen analyzes the sound your phone is playing, in real time, to animate the lights. The audio is not recorded, saved or sent to anyone. To do this the app uses the microphone permission, which Android requires for reading the audio output level, and, only if you choose it, Android’s screen-capture permission to capture audio from other apps. No screen content is captured or stored.
- What is playing. If you grant notification access, the app reads the track title, artist, album art and playback state from your music app’s media controls, to show them on the always-on screen, to pick colors from the album art, and to offer play, pause and skip. This stays on your device. The app does not read, store or send the content of your other notifications.
- Which app is in front. If you grant usage access, the app checks which app is open so it can hide the lights over full-screen apps or show them only over apps you chose. This stays on your device.
- Your settings. Your chosen designs, colors, saved presets, custom palettes and app lists are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
## Permissions and why
- Microphone: read the audio output level for the lights.
- Display over other apps: draw the edge lights above other apps.
- Notification access: read now-playing information (optional).
- Usage access: detect the app in front (optional).
- Notifications and foreground service: keep the lights running and let you stop them from the notification.
- Internet: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
## Ads
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares data such as your device’s IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), interactions with ads, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google’s consent form before requesting ads, and you can change it any time under Settings → Ad and privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Purchasing Orlumen Pro removes ads, and the ads SDK is then not started. See Google’s advertising policies.
## Purchases
Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. The app only receives whether you own Orlumen Pro. We never see your payment details.
## Children
Orlumen is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
## Sharing and retention
We do not sell your data and have no servers that receive it. The only third parties that receive data from the app are Google (ads and Google Play billing), as described above. Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete; uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device.
## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
