## Full description (max 4000)
Orlumen turns the music you play into live lights that flow around the edge of your screen, and into an always-on clock that shows what is playing.

EDGE LIGHTS
Six original designs: Filament, Tide, Strand, Rail, Ember and Aurora. Tune thickness, height, sensitivity, smoothing and direction, and adjust each design's own options. The lights follow your screen's rounded corners and respond to bass at the bottom and highs at the top.

ALWAYS-ON CLOCK
Eight original clocks: Orbit, Slab, Ledger, Meridian, Lexicon, Lunar, Duet and Bitmap. The clock appears at low brightness on a black background when the screen turns off, shows the date, battery and the track that is playing, and shifts slightly over time to protect your screen.

COLORS THAT FOLLOW THE MUSIC
Choose from ten stock palettes, build your own with the color wheel, match the album art of the playing track, or use your wallpaper's colors.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Show the lights over every app or only over apps you pick, hide them over games and video, dim the screen behind them, and start or stop from a Quick Settings tile or the notification.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Audio is analyzed live on your device. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Orlumen has no account and no analytics of its own.

FREE, WITH ONE OPTIONAL UPGRADE
Every design and feature is free. A single one-time purchase removes ads. No subscription.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Android does not offer third-party apps a built-in always-on display. Orlumen shows its own clock when the screen turns off, so it uses more battery than a system always-on display.
- The lights move with sound. If you are sensitive to flashing light, lower the sensitivity or stop the lights.
- Orlumen asks only for what its features need: microphone (to read the audio level), display over other apps (to draw the lights), and, optionally, notification access (now playing) and usage access (hide over full-screen apps).
