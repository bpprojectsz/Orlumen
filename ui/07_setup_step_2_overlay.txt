Draw over other apps
Orlumen draws the edge lights on top of other apps. Android asks you to allow this in Settings.
On the next screen, switch on “Allow display over other apps” for Orlumen. If Android shows a list of apps instead, find Orlumen in it (tip: tap the search icon).
Android can grey out this switch for apps installed outside the Play Store. If it says “App was denied access”, tap Open app info, press the three-dot menu, choose “Allow restricted settings”, then try again.
