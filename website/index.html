Your music, around the edge of your screen.

Orlumen turns whatever you play into live lights that follow your screen’s edge, plus an always-on clock that shows what’s playing. Everything is analyzed on your phone and nothing is recorded.

What you get

Six edge-light designs

Filament, Tide, Strand, Rail, Ember and Aurora. Tune thickness, height, sensitivity and direction.

Eight always-on clocks

Orbit, Slab, Ledger, Meridian, Lexicon, Lunar, Duet and Bitmap, with burn-in protection built in.

Colors that follow the music

Pick a palette, match the album art of the playing track, or use your wallpaper’s colors.

Private by design

Audio is processed live on your device. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Free, with one optional upgrade

Every design is free. A single one-time purchase removes ads for good. No subscription.

You stay in control

Choose which apps show the lights, hide them over games and video, and start or stop from a Quick Settings tile.

Good to know

Always-on clock

Android has no public always-on display for third-party apps. Orlumen shows its clock when the screen turns off, so battery use is higher than a built-in always-on display.

Flashing lights

The lights move with sound. If you are sensitive to flashing or flickering light, use low sensitivity or stop using the lights.
