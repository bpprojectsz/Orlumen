Support

Need help? Email stmakarios@gmail.com with your phone model, Android version and what you see. Screenshots help.

The lights do not appear

  • Open Orlumen and tap Start edge lights. Allow the microphone permission, then switch on Orlumen in the “Display over other apps” list.
  • Play music in any app. If the level on the Diagnostics screen stays at 0 while music plays, open Settings → Audio source and choose App audio capture.
  • Some phones stop background apps. Remove Orlumen from battery optimization in your phone’s settings.

Android says “App was denied access” or “Restricted setting”

This happens only for apps installed from a file instead of Google Play. Open Settings → Apps → Orlumen, tap the three-dot menu, choose Allow restricted settings, then try again. Copies installed from Google Play do not show this.

Album colors and now playing do not work

Turn on notification access for Orlumen in Android settings (Notification access). Orlumen only reads the playing track’s title, artist and artwork, on your device.

How the always-on clock works

Turn on Always-on display in Orlumen. When you press the power button the clock appears at low brightness on a black background. Tap the screen to return to your lock screen. Android does not let apps replace the system always-on display, so battery use is higher than a built-in one. The screensaver option in Settings is an alternative that runs mostly while charging.

Ads and Orlumen Pro

The free app shows ads. A one-time purchase removes them. If you bought Pro and ads still show, open Remove ads and tap Restore purchase. Change ad choices under Settings → Ad and privacy choices.

Safety

The lights move with sound. If flashing light bothers you, lower sensitivity in the design editor or stop the lights.
